# Market Watchlist

**An AI-powered market intelligence platform with smart alerts, market replay, and AI-driven stock analysis.**

## Overview

Market Watchlist is a market intelligence platform designed to help users monitor stocks, identify unusual price movements, analyze market behavior, and interact with market information through an integrated AI chatbot.

The platform combines historical stock-price analysis, statistical anomaly detection, benchmark comparisons, smart alerts, and conversational assistance to make market monitoring more informative and accessible.

## Key Features

- **Smart Alerts:** Highlight significant and notable stock movements based on configured detection criteria.
- **AI-Driven Stock Analysis:** Help users explore stock-related information and gain insights through AI-powered analysis.
- **Market Replay:** Review historical market activity to understand how stock movements developed over time.
- **Interactive AI Chatbot:** Ask questions and interact with the platform through a conversational interface.
- **Historical Price Analysis:** Compare recent stock movements against historical observations.
- **Anomaly Detection:** Use statistical measures such as z-scores to identify unusual price movements.
- **Benchmark Comparison:** Compare individual stock movements against benchmark performance.
- **Watchlist Monitoring:** Track selected stocks and identify changes since the previous visit.
- **Data Quality Handling:** Account for stale data and insufficient historical observations.

## Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Database Hosting:** Neon
- **Market Data:** Yahoo Finance data ingestion
- **Analysis:** Statistical analysis and anomaly detection
- **AI Interaction:** Integrated chatbot
